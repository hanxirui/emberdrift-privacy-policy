# Emberdrift Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 27, 2026**

Emberdrift is a fully offline, single-player card game. **We do not collect, store, process, or share any personal information.**

## 1. Information We Collect

None. The game does not require an account, does not ask for any personal data, and does not send any data anywhere.

## 2. Local Storage

Your game progress is saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

## 3. Third Parties, Ads and Network

The game contains no advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and does not communicate with any server. It is fully playable with no network connection.

## 4. Children's Privacy

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

## 5. Permissions

The app requests no sensitive device permissions.

## 6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer at **hanxirui@detadata.com**.

Developer: Han Xirui (韩锡锐)  
App: Emberdrift v1.0.0

# Emberdrift（余烬星海）隐私政策

**最后更新：2026 年 8 月 27 日**

Emberdrift 是一款完全离线的单机卡牌游戏。**我们不收集、存储、处理或共享任何个人信息。**

无。游戏不需要账号，不索取任何个人数据，也不向任何地方发送数据。

游戏不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。
